Understanding Urgent Care Services

A practical guide to choosing care for non-emergency health concerns.

When Might Urgent Care Be Appropriate?

Urgent care clinics may be an option for health concerns that need prompt attention but are not life-threatening. The right care setting depends on the symptoms, their severity, and the person's medical needs.

Preparing for a Visit

Before visiting a clinic, consider bringing identification, your insurance information if applicable, a list of medicines, and relevant medical details. Check the clinic's current services and hours before travelling.

Finding a Local Urgent Care Clinic

When looking for local care, review the clinic's published services, location, and contact information. For example, you can visit Sahara West Urgent Care to learn more about the clinic and its available services.

When to Seek Emergency Help

Urgent care is not a substitute for emergency treatment. For potentially life-threatening symptoms, such as severe difficulty breathing or signs of a stroke, call 911 in the United States.
